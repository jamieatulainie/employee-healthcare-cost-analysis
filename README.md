# Employee Healthcare Cost Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes employee healthcare costs to identify health and lifestyle factors associated with higher annual medical expenditures.

The analysis focuses on demographic characteristics, smoking status, mental health, chronic diseases, BMI, and their relationship with medical costs.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to present the key findings and allow users to explore the data by region, gender, and age group.


## Dataset

- **Dataset:** Medical Insurance Dataset
- **Source:** Kaggle
- **Original records:** 100,000
- **Original columns:** 54
- **Cleaned records:** 97,720
- **Selected columns:** 30


## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- MySQL
- Power BI

## Data Preparation

The dataset was cleaned and prepared before analysis. The process included checking for duplicate records, handling outliers, reviewing data types, and selecting relevant columns for the analysis.

## Analysis

The analysis focused on the following areas:

- Age and gender in relation to annual medical costs
- Smoking status and medical costs
- Mental health and medical costs
- Prevalence of chronic diseases
- BMI, smoking status, and medical costs

## Key Findings

- Current smokers had the highest average annual medical costs.
- Employees with mental health conditions had higher average medical costs.
- Hypertension was the most prevalent chronic condition, followed by arthritis and diabetes.
- Older employees showed higher average annual medical costs.

## Recommendations

- Implement smoking cessation and health awareness programs.
- Provide mental health support and employee wellness initiatives.
- Strengthen screening and disease management programs for common chronic conditions.
- Consider targeted health interventions for higher-risk employee groups.

## Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard includes:

- Key performance indicators for medical costs
- Medical cost analysis by age group
- Chronic disease prevalence
- Mental health and medical costs
- BMI and medical costs by smoking status
- Interactive filters for region, gender, and age group

## Project Note

This analysis highlights associations between employee characteristics, health conditions, lifestyle factors, and medical costs. The findings indicate patterns in the data but do not establish causal relationships.
